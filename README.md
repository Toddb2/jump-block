# Jump Block

Offline training app for the 2026–27 winter jump block: daily plan, gym logging with periodised
heavy and deload weeks, guided mobility routines with timers, weigh-ins and progress.

Open https://toddb2.github.io/jump-block/ on your phone and add it to your home screen.
All training data stays on the device (back it up from the Plan tab).

When you change any file, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` so installed copies pick up the update.
